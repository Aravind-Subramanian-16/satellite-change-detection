Known Limitations
  • Cloud Cover: Clouds and shadows can obscure the ground and may occasionally be detected as false changes.
  • Resolution Limits: Satellite imagery is limited to 10m/pixel. Very small changes (like individual trees or cars) will not be visible.
  • Seasonal Changes: Natural seasonal variations in vegetation or agriculture may sometimes be misread as land-use change.
