See what changed on the ground.

Pick a place, choose two dates, and this app compares free satellite photos to show where the land changed — farmland, new buildings, forest loss, water and more.

  • Clouds and shadows are masked out automatically
  • Plain-English summary, change map, and downloads
  • Every result carries an honesty note and a data-quality check

Results are an automated estimate, not ground truth. Check important findings on the ground.

This app compares two satellite images of the same place and flags pixels where the spectral rules changed. It is a time-saving pointer, not a survey:

  • It works on free 10 m Sentinel-2 imagery, so anything smaller than about 10 m (individual buildings, most roads) is usually missed.
  • Change labels like “New buildings” or “Forest loss” are guesses from colour patterns; clouds, shadows, wet soil and harvest can be mistaken for change.
  • Only the built-up profile has been tested on a labelled dataset (one urban dataset, moderate scores). Farmland, forest, water and general detection are estimates, not validated.
  • Always confirm important findings with a site visit, local knowledge, or higher-resolution imagery.
