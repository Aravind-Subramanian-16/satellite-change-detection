Sentinel-2 L2A · 10 m · Copernicus Data Space

See what changed on the ground.

Choose an area on the map, compare two dates of Sentinel-2 imagery, and review the spectral evidence before you rely on it. Change labels are transparent heuristics, not a validated land-cover classifier. Decision support, not ground truth.

  • Cloud and shadow masking with visible masked area
  • Scene ranking, seasonal warnings, and minimum mapping units
  • GeoJSON, CSV, and PDF exports with provenance

Step 3

Choose scenes and run

Candidates are ranked by AOI coverage, catalog cloud cover, and date proximity. AOI cloud fraction is measured after download; low catalog cloud does not guarantee a clear AOI.

Downloading imagery and computing indices can take up to a minute. Nothing is written to Copernicus.
