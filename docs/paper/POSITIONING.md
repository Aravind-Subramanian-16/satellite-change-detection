# Positioning against published work

The manuscript positions itself as a bounded single-model case study, not as a
new detector or a ranking of published methods. The related-work section cites
foundational Siamese, attention/transformer, spectral-index, thresholding and
OSCD sources, but does not claim protocol-matched comparative performance.

Do not add published metric comparisons unless the original paper and exact
protocol are checked. Dataset, split, labels, preprocessing and metric must
match; otherwise report the comparison as not comparable. In particular, do
not compare this paper's previously scored OSCD test cities with another
method's score as though it were a controlled head-to-head result.
