## 2. The problem

**A native-resolution score does not travel to 10 m**

- The checkpoint is trained and scored at 0.5 m on LEVIR-CD aerial pairs.
- Sentinel-2 provides 10 m and 20 m bands, free and frequently revisited.
- Same model, coarser pixel support: small objects, boundaries and mixed pixels
  change.
- Question: what happens to this checkpoint when the same scenes are coarsened?

Scale card: one 10 m Sentinel-2 pixel (large square) contains a 0.5 m LEVIR-CD
pixel (orange square) at 1/20 per side; 20x coarser linearly, 400x the area.

## 3. Design

**One frozen model, two evaluations, no retraining**

Controlled degradation:
- 2,048 LEVIR-CD test pairs.
- Six factors: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 20.
- Gaussian blur + LANCZOS down/up.
- Same scene content; only sampling changes.

Real Sentinel-2 (OSCD):
- 10 official test cities.
- 3,077,936 pixels; 5.17% changed.
- Zero-shot CNN; NDVI/NDBI and Otsu rules.
- No SCL; confounded with sensor and season.

Footer: Frozen checkpoint, one architecture, one training run, fixed 0.5
threshold.

## 4. Degradation protocol

**Coarsen, then upsample back to 256x256**

1. 256x256 before/after pair.
2. Gaussian blur, radius factor/2.
3. LANCZOS downsample to (256/factor)^2.
4. LANCZOS upsample back to 256x256.

- Factors 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 20 -> 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 10 m.
- At 10 m: a 12x12 intermediate for a 256x256 input.
- ImageNet normalization, threshold 0.5, pooled TP/FP/FN over 2,048 pairs.
- This isolates blur and resampling. It is not a Sentinel-2 simulation.

## 5. Result

**F1 falls from 87.06% to 4.41% by 2 m**

| Resolution | Pooled F1 |
|---|---:|
| 0.5 m | 87.06% |
| 1 m | 70.81% (-16.25) |
| 2 m | 4.41% |
| 8-10 m | 2.61% |

Recall: 85.36% -> 1.32%. The decline is recall-dominated. Pooled counts; one
checkpoint.

## 6. Why precision misleads

**At 10 m, the model predicts almost nothing**

- 0.068% of pixels predicted changed: 91,006 of 134,217,728.
- 924 / 935 changed images with zero predicted change (98.82%).
- 99.47% precision, from 90,527 TP and 479 FP; the mask is nearly empty.

Bottom line: high precision means the mask is nearly empty, not that
localisation is good.

## 8. City-level variation

**Pooled scores hide large, inconsistent city differences**

- Percentile F1 range: 0.0005 (saclay_w) - 0.2481 (chongqing).
- Hardcoded F1 range: 0.0032 (dubai) - 0.3102 (chongqing).
- Random (analytic): 0.0517.
- CNN joint: zero predictions in 8 of 10 cities.

Descriptive ranges; ten cities. Intervals resample cities or pixels, not
training runs.

## 9. Interpretation

**Test the resolution path you will deploy**

1. Test the deployed resolution path. A native-resolution benchmark score does
   not reveal coarse-resolution failure.
2. Report precision with predicted fraction and prevalence. 99.47% precision
   meant 0.068% of pixels predicted changed.
3. Do not treat simple indices as a validated fallback. Both spectral rules
   under-predict pooled change; Otsu predicts 67.35% changed.

The paper identifies a risk in evaluation, not a new detector.

## 10. What this does not show

**One checkpoint, one synthetic degradation, a used test split**

- One architecture and one training run; no retraining or seed variation.
- Synthetic degradation: no spectra, atmosphere, mixed pixels or noise.
- OSCD confounds resolution with sensor, geography and season; no SCL.
- Ten cities; the test split had been scored before the reported run.
- The deployed app and scene selection are not validated end to end.

Claim boundary: no causal claim about the real Sentinel-2 gap; no priority
claim; no operational claim. The paper reports descriptive evidence, not a
validated system.

## 11. Next evidence

**What would make the result stronger**

1. A second architecture and checkpoint, with multiple seeds.
2. Real same-scene acquisitions at multiple resolutions.
3. An untouched city set or a new dataset.
4. An SCL-preserving benchmark and a wider threshold grid.

An operator study on whether warnings change decisions. These are evidence
needs, not results.

## 12. Takeaway

**A 0.5 m score is not a 10 m validation.**

- 87.06% -> 4.41% pooled F1 from 0.5 m to 2 m under blur + resampling.
- 0.068% of pixels predicted changed at 10 m (91,006 of 134,217,728).
- 924 / 935 changed images with zero predicted change (98.82%).
- Real OSCD: CNN F1 0.0007; spectral rules 0.078-0.118; neither is a validated
  fallback.
